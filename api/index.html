Our Mission

Built for civic journalists and community advocates

Civic Resilience Network aggregates news from national outlets, government agencies, and local Iowa sources — surfacing the stories that matter most to communities, often before they trend.

We track emergency alerts, local government decisions, housing policy, public health, and civic engagement stories — then make them accessible for community broadcasters and local newsrooms.

Multi-Source Coverage

Stories tracked across 15+ sources. When multiple outlets cover the same story, we show you who and highlight what's being missed.

Blindspot Detection

Stories covered by only one source type get flagged. These are often the most important local stories flying under the radar.
